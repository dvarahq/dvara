# Contributor Licence Agreement

Thank you for contributing to DVARA. This agreement clarifies the intellectual
property licence granted with contributions from any person or entity.

**It is not asked for on every contribution.** Contributions are accepted under
Apache-2.0, whose section 5 already licenses anything submitted for inclusion.
DVARA Labs asks for a signature on substantial contributions, at its discretion,
and will say so on the pull request.

**You keep your copyright.** Section 2 is a licence, not an assignment — nothing
here transfers ownership of your work.

You accept these terms by signing as described in [How to sign](#how-to-sign),
which also sets out what this agreement adds beyond the rights Apache-2.0 already
grants when you open a pull request.

## Corporate Contributor Licence Agreement

Where contributions are made in the course of employment, the employer accepts
this agreement **in addition to** the individual agreement above — not instead of
it. Each developer still signs individually. The corporate agreement settles what
the employer owns; the individual one covers what the employer does not, and the
two answer different questions.

The terms of sections 1 through 8 apply to the Corporation, with the following
additions:

**Schedule of authorised contributors.** The Corporation names the employees
authorised to submit Contributions on its behalf, and undertakes to keep that
list current. Contributions from a person not on the list are not covered by
this agreement.

**Scope.** This agreement covers Contributions submitted by the named employees
in the course of their employment. It does not cover their personal
contributions, which are governed by the individual agreement.

**To execute this, [open an issue](https://github.com/dvarahq/dvara/issues)
saying you need a corporate agreement**, or write to
[support@dvarahq.com](mailto:support@dvarahq.com). Do not put the schedule of
employees in a public issue — we will reply with where to send it.

## How to sign

**Signing is not automated yet.** Post this sentence as a comment on your pull
request:

```
I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA
```

A maintainer records it before merging. There is no bot, no email and no
paperwork, and you are asked once.

**You may contribute before any of this exists.** Apache-2.0 section 5 already
provides that a contribution submitted for inclusion is under the terms of that
licence unless you say otherwise, and section 2 of that licence includes the
right to sublicense. So a contribution is usable without this agreement. What
this agreement adds is narrower: the ability to relicense the project itself
later, and your representation that you are entitled to grant what you are
granting.

**Corporations** [open an issue](https://github.com/dvarahq/dvara/issues) as
described above. A schedule of named employees has to be agreed with us directly.

## Questions

[Open an issue](https://github.com/dvarahq/dvara/issues). If something in this
agreement is unclear or seems wrong, say so — that is useful feedback and not an
imposition.
