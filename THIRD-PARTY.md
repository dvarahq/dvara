## Why this file exists

Until this scan the dependency tree had been checked **by artifact name only**. Names look uniformly
Apache-2.0 and MIT, and an MPL, CDDL, EPL or copyleft artifact with an innocuous name would not have
been noticed. Distributing one inside an Apache-2.0 artifact is cheap to fix now and impossible to
fix after a permanent Maven Central release.
